# Yourterms

**AI-powered behavioral investing platform designed to help investors make better decisions — not simply more trades.**

Yourterms explores how AI and behavioral finance can improve the decision-making layer of retail investing.

Rather than recommending what investors should buy or sell, Yourterms helps users understand **why they are considering a decision**, identify potential behavioral biases, evaluate relevant information, and make decisions aligned with their own goals.

---

## The Problem

Retail investing has become dramatically more accessible, but better access does not always result in better decisions.

Investors increasingly face:

- Information overload
- FOMO and performance chasing
- Panic selling and loss aversion
- Overconfidence
- Conflicting financial opinions
- Limited personalization from existing investing platforms

Most brokerage products are optimized around **access, information, and execution**.

Yourterms focuses on the layer before execution:

> **How can we help investors make better decisions in the first place?**

---

## Product Vision

**Help every investor make decisions on their terms — not their emotions.**

Yourterms combines AI, behavioral finance, and personalized context to create an investing companion that helps users think through investment decisions rather than simply generating recommendations.

The goal is not to replace investor judgment.

**The goal is to improve it.**

---

## How It Works

A user might ask:

> *"I'm thinking about selling this stock because it's down 20%. What should I consider?"*

Instead of returning a simple **Buy / Hold / Sell** recommendation, Yourterms can:

1. Understand the investor's goal and reasoning
2. Identify potential behavioral biases
3. Surface relevant information
4. Explain risks and trade-offs
5. Challenge assumptions
6. Help the investor reach their own informed decision

This creates a continuous loop:

**Understand → Decide → Execute → Learn**

---

## Why Now?

Three shifts create the opportunity for Yourterms:

**Generative AI**  
Personalized financial conversations can now happen at unprecedented scale.

**Democratization of Investing**  
Commission-free trading and mobile brokerages have dramatically lowered barriers to investing.

**Information Overload**  
Investors increasingly need help interpreting information rather than simply accessing more of it.

Together, these trends create an opportunity for a new category:

> **AI-powered behavioral decision intelligence for investing.**

---

## Strategic Opportunity

Existing products largely compete around different parts of the investing journey:

| Category | Primary Value |
| --- | --- |
| Robinhood / Webull | Trading & execution |
| Fidelity / Schwab | Brokerage, research & wealth management |
| Betterment / Wealthfront | Automated investing |
| Financial Media | Information |
| General-Purpose AI | Answers & conversation |
| **Yourterms** | **Behavioral intelligence & decision quality** |

### Key Market Insight

**The previous generation of fintech democratized access to investing.**

**Yourterms explores what happens when AI democratizes better investment decision-making.**

## Product Principles

1. **Improve decisions, not trading volume**
2. **Explain, don't just recommend**
3. **Challenge assumptions**
4. **Personalize using context**
5. **Treat behavior as a product signal**
6. **Make AI reasoning understandable**
7. **Keep the investor in control**

---

## North Star

> **Help users make more thoughtful investment decisions than they would have made alone.**

Potential measures include:

- Decision confidence before vs. after using Yourterms
- Behavioral biases identified
- Decisions reconsidered after reflection
- Repeat decision-analysis sessions
- User-reported improvement in investing discipline

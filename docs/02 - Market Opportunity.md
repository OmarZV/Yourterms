# 02 - Market Opportunity

## Executive Summary

Retail investing platforms have dramatically reduced the friction required to access financial markets. Commission-free trading, fractional shares, mobile-first brokerages, and automated investing have made it easier than ever for individuals to invest.

However, easier access has not necessarily resulted in better investment decisions.

Investors still face behavioral challenges including fear of missing out, panic selling, overconfidence, loss aversion, performance chasing, and excessive trading. Existing platforms primarily compete on **execution, access, financial products, and information**, leaving an opportunity for a product focused on the **quality of the decisions investors make before they transact**.

**Yourterms is positioned around this behavioral decision layer: using AI to help investors understand their own behavior, evaluate decisions, and invest on their terms rather than reactively to the market.**

---

## Market Opportunity

Yourterms sits at the intersection of three large markets:

### 1. Retail Investing

Millions of individuals manage investments directly through digital brokerage platforms such as Robinhood, Fidelity, Charles Schwab, and SoFi.

These platforms have successfully democratized access to investing, but their core experiences remain centered around accounts, assets, research, and transaction execution.

> **Opportunity:** Build intelligence around the decision that occurs *before* the trade.

### 2. Digital Financial Advice

Traditional financial advice has historically been expensive and inaccessible to many retail investors. Robo-advisors expanded access by automating portfolio construction, but much of the experience remains portfolio-centric rather than behavior-centric.

Generative AI creates the possibility of delivering individualized guidance conversationally and at dramatically lower marginal cost.

> **Opportunity:** Move from standardized portfolio advice toward personalized, contextual investing guidance.

### 3. Generative AI for Personal Finance

LLMs create a new interface for financial products: conversation.

Instead of navigating research reports, charts, educational articles, and account screens independently, investors can potentially ask questions, explore trade-offs, and receive explanations tailored to their goals and level of financial knowledge.

> **Opportunity:** Become the behavioral intelligence layer between financial information and investor action.

---

## TAM — Total Addressable Market

The broadest opportunity includes self-directed and digitally engaged retail investors who could benefit from AI-assisted investing guidance.

The long-term opportunity spans:

- Digital brokerage customers
- Self-directed investors
- Robo-advisory customers
- Financial information and research users
- Consumers using AI for financial questions
- Investors paying for premium financial tools and advice

The long-term TAM therefore extends beyond a standalone investing application toward the broader **digital wealth management and financial guidance market**.

---

## SAM — Serviceable Available Market

The initial serviceable market is:

> **Digitally active U.S. retail investors who manage some or all of their investments themselves and regularly use digital tools to research or make investment decisions.**

Particularly attractive segments include:

- New and intermediate investors
- Millennials and Gen Z investors
- Mobile-first brokerage users
- Investors without a dedicated financial advisor
- Investors managing multiple brokerage accounts
- Investors seeking greater confidence in their decisions

This population already demonstrates a willingness to use technology for investing but remains underserved by personalized behavioral guidance.

---

## SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market

Yourterms' initial obtainable market should focus on a specific wedge rather than attempting to replace an investor's brokerage.

### Initial Beachhead

> **Self-directed investors who understand the fundamentals of investing but struggle with confidence, discipline, or emotionally driven decisions.**

Instead of asking users to move their assets, Yourterms can initially exist alongside their existing brokerage.

This substantially reduces adoption friction.

The initial product does not need to replace Robinhood, Schwab, Fidelity, or SoFi.

**It needs to make their customers better investors.**

---

## Why Now?

Several structural changes make this opportunity particularly compelling:

- **Generative AI** enables personalized financial conversations at a scale that previously required human advisors.
- **Commission-free brokerage** has largely commoditized trade execution, shifting potential differentiation toward intelligence and experience.
- **Retail participation** has created a generation of self-directed investors with unprecedented access to sophisticated financial products.
- **Information abundance** has created a new problem: investors increasingly need help interpreting information rather than simply accessing it.
- **Behavioral finance research** demonstrates that investor psychology can materially influence financial decision-making.

Together, these trends create an opportunity to move the investing experience from:

**Access → Information → Execution**

to:

**Understand → Decide → Execute → Learn**

## Market Thesis

> **The next generation of investing products will compete not only on where investors trade or what information they can access, but on how effectively technology helps them make decisions.**

Yourterms' opportunity is to own that layer.

Rather than optimizing for more trades, more screen time, or more financial products, Yourterms can optimize for:

- **Better-informed decisions**
- **More disciplined investing behavior**
- **Greater investor confidence**
- **Improved understanding of risk and trade-offs**

### Strategic Position

**Yourterms is not another brokerage. It is the AI-powered behavioral decision layer that sits between investment information and investor action.**

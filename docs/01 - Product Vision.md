# 01 - Product Vision

## Vision

**Help every investor make decisions on their terms—not their emotions.**

Yourterms is an AI-powered investing companion designed to improve the quality of investment decisions by combining personalized guidance, behavioral insights, and financial education.

Rather than helping users trade more, Yourterms helps them **understand more before they trade**.

---

## The Problem

Investing has never been more accessible.

Commission-free trading, fractional shares, financial media, social networks, and mobile brokerages have given individual investors unprecedented access to financial markets.

But access has created a new challenge: **more information does not necessarily lead to better decisions.**

Investors must navigate:

- Fear of missing out (FOMO)
- Panic selling
- Loss aversion
- Overconfidence
- Performance chasing
- Information overload
- Conflicting financial opinions
- Difficulty separating emotion from investment strategy

Most investing platforms are designed to help users **execute a decision once it has been made**.

Yourterms focuses on what happens **before that decision**.

---

## The Product

Yourterms provides an intelligent layer between **investment information and investor action**.

A user can bring an investment decision to Yourterms:

> *"I'm thinking about selling this stock because it's down 20%. What should I consider?"*

Instead of simply producing a buy or sell recommendation, Yourterms helps the investor work through the decision.

The experience can:

1. **Understand** the investor's goals, time horizon, portfolio, and reasoning.
2. **Identify** behavioral biases that may be influencing the decision.
3. **Contextualize** relevant financial information.
4. **Explain** potential risks, trade-offs, and alternatives.
5. **Challenge** assumptions through personalized questions.
6. **Help** the investor reach a more informed decision.

The goal is not to replace investor judgment.

**The goal is to improve it.**

---

## Core Value Proposition

> **Yourterms helps self-directed investors make more informed, disciplined, and confident investment decisions through personalized AI guidance.**

Traditional brokerages answer:

**"How do I make this trade?"**

Financial media answers:

**"What's happening in the market?"**

Search engines answer:

**"What information exists about this investment?"**

Yourterms aims to answer:

**"Given my goals and reasoning, what should I think about before making this decision?"**

---

## Target User

Yourterms is initially designed for **self-directed retail investors** who:

- Manage their own investments
- Already understand basic investing concepts
- Research investments independently
- Use digital brokerage platforms
- Sometimes struggle with uncertainty or emotionally driven decisions
- Want guidance without completely delegating their portfolio to an advisor or robo-advisor

These investors do not necessarily need someone to make decisions for them.

They need better tools for **making decisions themselves**.

---

## Product Principles

### 1. Improve Decisions, Not Trading Volume

Success should come from helping users make better decisions—not encouraging more transactions.

### 2. Explain, Don't Just Recommend

Every AI-generated insight should help the user understand **why** something matters.

### 3. Challenge the Investor

The product should not simply agree with the user's assumptions.

When appropriate, Yourterms should surface alternative interpretations, risks, and behavioral biases.

### 4. Personalization Requires Context

Investment guidance should reflect the user's:

- Goals
- Time horizon
- Risk tolerance
- Portfolio
- Previous decisions
- Investing experience

### 5. Behavior Matters

Investing decisions are influenced by psychology as much as information.

Behavioral investing should therefore be a core product capability rather than an educational add-on.

### 6. Trust Must Be Earned

Financial AI requires transparency.

Users should understand:

- What information influenced an insight
- What assumptions were made
- Where uncertainty exists
- When information may be incomplete

### 7. Keep the Investor in Control

AI should augment human judgment rather than replace it.

The final decision always belongs to the investor.

---

## North Star

### Better Investment Decisions

Yourterms should ultimately measure success by whether users believe the product helped them make a **more thoughtful investment decision than they would have made alone**.

Supporting indicators could include:

- Decisions reconsidered after behavioral reflection
- Users identifying a behavioral bias
- Decision confidence before vs. after using Yourterms
- Repeat decision-analysis sessions
- Percentage of AI explanations users find useful
- User-reported improvement in investing discipline

---

## What Yourterms Is Not

Yourterms is **not**:

- A brokerage
- A stock-picking service
- A trading signal platform
- A replacement for professional financial advice
- A product designed to maximize trading frequency
- An AI that blindly tells investors what to buy or sell

Instead, Yourterms is designed to become the **decision intelligence layer for self-directed investing**.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Today, investors interact with financial products primarily through accounts, charts, research, news, and transaction interfaces.

The long-term opportunity is to introduce a new layer:

**Investor → Information → Yourterms → Decision → Brokerage**

Over time, Yourterms could develop an understanding of how an individual investor thinks, including their goals, investing philosophy, historical decisions, recurring biases, and responses to different market environments.

This creates the potential for an AI investing companion that does more than answer questions.

It can help investors recognize patterns in **their own decision-making**.

---

## Vision Statement

> **Yourterms exists to make high-quality investment thinking accessible to every self-directed investor—helping people understand their behavior, challenge their assumptions, and make financial decisions with greater confidence and discipline.**

### From Better Access to Better Decisions

The previous generation of fintech democratized **access to investing**.

**Yourterms aims to democratize better investment decision-making.**
